Auto-Aktivierung

Automatically activate all inactive PAYBACK coupons for each store you visit.

For example, visiting dm.de starts activation of all its inactive coupons, including in-store offers. Already activated coupons are skipped.

Coupons are activated one at a time. You can keep browsing while a batch finishes. Turning this off stops the batch after any request already in progress.

Auto-Aktivierung never redeems coupons, redirects you to a store, or places an order. Special coupons may still need action on PAYBACK.
